ABOUT ME
Hi! My name is Ender, or EnderSlime. I’m an illustrator, animator, writer, cosplayer, video editor… puppeteer… project and zine manager… merch designer… um. Yeah! I do a lot of things! Currently, I’m most well known for my projects in the Homestuck fandom, whether that be my large scale post-canon story Cookiefish or my merchandise like the Captchalogue Card ita bag.
This is my website, where you’ll find links to all of my projects. You’ll also find my portfolio and commission rates, links to all of my social media accounts, my merch store and Ko-fi where you can support me monthly, and news about my next projects. There are other fun things as well, like a page for the projects my friends make, and some other secret buttons! Feel free to explore and look around!
I also run a long term ongoing webcomic with my friends called Out of Game, which runs concurrently with its sister comic SAVE: Game. Both can be read on MSPFA, and most of my other projects fuel our making of this one!
I collaborate with others on my projects most of the time- none of my work would be any good if I made it all entirely by myself. That is my favorite way to learn, from others, and that's why I make art- to learn. This website has many coding contributions by FlaringK and Miao and would not exist without them. Thank you!
Feel free to check me out on any of these social media platforms- my work spans all of them! For any inquiries about commissions or other opportunities, please email me at:
