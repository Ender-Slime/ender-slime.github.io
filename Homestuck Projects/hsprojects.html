THEY CALL ME MISTER HOMESTUCK

I've helmed quite a few projects during my time in this fandom! Here are the highlights from each major project.

> The Cookiefish Timeline
The Cookiefish Timeline is my own personal Post Canon set of projects. It consists of substories focused on each character, all in different artistic mediums to fit the story. The most popular story is it's very first one- Jane and Dirk's Bombastic Baking Bonanza, from SAHCon 2023!

> Zines
I host various group and solo zines, both in the traditional "random assortment of stuff" way, and in the more organized collaborative print way. Whether I do limited print runs for a project or not, the digital PDFs always remain free. Always!

> Analog Horror
I have a Homestuck "analog horror" project hosted on YouTube, focusing on the perspective of the people of earth as the meteors fell. It was initially conceptualized in a video on my own channel called "meteors.mp4", and later remade and expanded to be bigger and better. Much better. And scarier!

> Merchandise
I do fanmerch for a living, and I'm most recognizable as the creator of the Captchalogue Card Ita Bags! Along with apparel, I also create handmade merch, and I am extremely proud to be able to bring joy to all of you through my stuff.
