My friends and I produce two MSFPAs, called SAVE: Game and Out Of Game ! They are separate comics following two halves of the same story, and reading both gives you the full picture. Me and Moony's years long forever passion project. :]
A story about a troll trying to save the world not only for themselves, but for their friends. Follow the hijinks, heartbreak, and plethora of homosexual feelings as these trolls break the world just to be happy again.
A story about some trolls, an alien that crash lands in their backyard, and the hijinks that ensue. Follow along as they run from cops, suppress homosexual feelings, and figure out a way to get their alien friend back home.
